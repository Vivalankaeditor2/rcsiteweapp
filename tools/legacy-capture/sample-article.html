Court Rejects Shiranthi's Bail Application

Sunday, October 11, 2026 - VivaLanka.com

The Maligakanda Magistrate's Court recently rejected the anticipatory bail application filed by Shiranthi Rajapaksa. The application was submitted as a preventive measure against her potential arrest by the Financial Crimes Investigation Division, which is investigating alleged financial irregularities linked to the Siriliya Saviya account.

During the hearing, the court considered arguments from both the defense and the prosecution. The defense maintained that the arrest was unwarranted, while the prosecution argued that her presence was necessary for the ongoing investigation. After reviewing the submissions, the court decided to reject the bail request, emphasizing the importance of a thorough legal process.

Additionally, the court issued a notice to the head of the Financial Crimes Investigation Division, instructing them to appear before the court and provide a detailed report on the investigation. This move aims to ensure transparency and accountability in the proceedings. The case has drawn significant public attention due to the high-profile individuals involved and its broader implications for financial accountability in Sri Lanka.
